### Overview

This week covers the two skills every other week in this course depends on: a repeatable way to diagnose a problem instead of guessing at it, and a ticket note detailed enough that the next person, possibly you in three months, can pick up exactly where you left off.

Neither one is glamorous. Both are what separates a technician who gets trusted with harder tickets from one who stays stuck resetting passwords all day.

The week closes with a real ticket: a user locked out right after changing their password. You will work it the way it actually shows up on a queue, symptom first, answer last, not with the fix handed to you up front.
