### Finding Users and Computers

Most tickets start with a name, so the first skill on the desk is finding the right account quickly. The **Find** dialog does that far faster than scrolling through OUs. In Active Directory Users and Computers, right-click the domain or any OU and choose **Find**.

Then pick what you're looking for from the dropdown:

- Users, Contacts, and Groups for a person
- Computers for a machine

Either way, you can search by name or by username.

Try it now with `jordan.ellis`, the Staff Accountant in Finance and Accounting. You'll see the account turn up in seconds, whichever OU you started from.

It's worth getting comfortable with Find early, because you'll use it on most tickets. Remember, though, that finding the object is only the start. Once it's open, read its folder and then its Member Of tab, just as you learned on the earlier tabs.
