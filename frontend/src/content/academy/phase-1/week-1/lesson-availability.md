### Availability

**Availability is posted hours. Open when customers need the branch.** It keeps systems and data there when people need them, the same job a website does when it loads on time.

A thing needs availability when *not being there on time* is the harm:

- email on Monday
- the time clock
- the VPN
- the payment page at checkout

When availability fails, the vault can be locked and every seal intact. If the doors never open, the branch still failed, because the work stops. That failure is a **lockout**.

Backups, spare servers and patching keep the hours. A DDoS attack is a branch that never unlocks.
