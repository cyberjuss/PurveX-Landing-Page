### How It All Connects

A vulnerability gives a threat a way in. If the threat succeeds, confidentiality, integrity or availability fails, sometimes more than one. Risk measures how likely that is and how much it would cost, before anything happens.

Work through it in this order:

- the vulnerability
- the threat
- the CIA property that would fail
- the impact

### Trade-offs

The three properties compete. Stronger confidentiality, such as extra MFA or an air gap, can reduce availability. Higher availability, such as an open file share, can weaken confidentiality.

Decide which property matters most for the data, then choose the control.
