### How It All Connects

1. A **vulnerability** exists (unpatched server).
2. A **threat** (attacker) targets that vulnerability.
3. If successful, the attack opens the vault, breaks the seal, and/or closes the branch — **confidentiality, integrity, and/or availability**.
4. The **risk** was the probability and impact of that happening, and it existed before the attack ever occurred. This is exactly why security teams assess and prioritize risk *before* an incident, not after. The risk was always there to be found.
