### Overview

Before you write a ticket or close an alert name what failed. Calling something secure is not enough because it is one of three jobs holding.

A thing needs the vault when seeing it is the harm. It needs the seal when a silent change is the harm. It needs the hours when not being there on time is the harm. Name the downside first and you know which job the control is doing:

- leak
- lie
- lockout

That is the CIA triad and you already meet it on the desk. Each of these is a different failure and each one needs a different response:

- a leaked password
- a payroll number that quietly changed
- a site that will not load

The rest of this week is how analysts talk about risk. A vulnerability and a threat are two different things and neither creates danger on its own. Risk shows up only when they combine so split them cleanly and you can prioritize a queue instead of reacting to every word that sounds scary.

Your job this week is to look at a failure and say which job broke then say whether the danger is a weakness, an actor, or the chance those two meet.
