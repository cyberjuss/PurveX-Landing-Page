### Overview

"Secure" is not a finding. Before you write a ticket or close an alert, name what actually failed.

Security comes down to three jobs: keeping things private, keeping them accurate, and keeping them available. Each job has its own kind of failure:

- A leak, when the wrong person sees something
- A lie, when something changes silently
- A lockout, when something is not there when needed

Together those three jobs are the CIA triad, and you already meet it on the desk. A leaked password, a payroll number that quietly changed, and a site that will not load are three different failures. Each needs a different response.

The rest of this week covers how analysts talk about risk. A vulnerability and a threat are two different things, and neither creates danger on its own.

Risk appears only when the two combine. Once you can separate them cleanly, you can prioritize a queue instead of reacting to every word that sounds scary.

Your job this week is to look at a failure and say which job broke. Then say whether the danger is a weakness, an actor, or the chance that those two meet.
