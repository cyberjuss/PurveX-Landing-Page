### Overview

Calling a system "secure" tells your team nothing it can act on. Before you write a ticket or close an alert, name the part that failed.

Security comes down to three jobs: keeping things private, keeping them accurate, and keeping them available. Each job has its own kind of failure:

- A leak, when the wrong person sees something
- A lie, when something changes silently
- A lockout, when something is not there when needed

Together those three jobs are the CIA triad, and you already meet it on the desk.

A leaked password and a silently changed payroll number are different failures, and a site that will not load is a third. Each needs a different response.

This week also covers how analysts talk about risk. A vulnerability and a threat are two different things, and risk exists only where the two meet.

Once you can tell them apart, you can rank a queue instead of reacting to every word that sounds scary.

This week you look at a failure and say which job broke. Then say whether the danger is a weakness or an actor, or the chance that the two meet.
