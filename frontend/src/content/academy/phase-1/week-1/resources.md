### Sources for this week's facts

- **Confidentiality, integrity and availability, and what losing each one means (disclosure, modification, disruption):** [NIST FIPS 199: Standards for Security Categorization](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/FIPS/NIST.FIPS.199.pdf)
- **A vulnerability is a weakness a threat source could exploit:** [NIST glossary: vulnerability](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/vulnerability)
- **A threat is any circumstance or event that could cause harm:** [NIST glossary: threat](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/threat)
- **Risk combines the likelihood of an event with its impact:** [NIST glossary: risk](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/risk) and [NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1: Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/Legacy/SP/nistspecialpublication800-30r1.pdf)
- **CVSS adjusts a score to what the asset holds (Confidentiality, Integrity and Availability Requirements):** [FIRST: CVSS v3.1 specification](https://www.first.org/cvss/v3-1/specification-document)
- **A denial-of-service attack stops legitimate users reaching a system:** [CISA: Understanding Denial-of-Service Attacks](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/news/understanding-denial-service-attacks)
