### The Update Nobody Can Vouch For

**Situation:** IT released VPN update 2.4.1 and posted its SHA-256 hash. Three copies of the update are now going around PurveX Financial, and staff want to know which ones are safe to install.

**Task:** Hash each copy with CyberChef or the command line and compare it with the hash IT published. Find the copy that does not match and spot what was changed inside it. Then decide what happens next.

The files are plain text, so nothing in them can run.
