### The Leaked Password Table

**Situation:** Eight PurveX staff used a vendor portal called LedgerLine. The portal was breached and its user table is now online. Over the years the vendor stored the same passwords four different ways, so the dump holds four generations of each account.

**Your task:** Work out what each generation gives away and what PurveX should do first. Anyone can reverse encoding and encryption needs the key. Hashing is one-way, but weak passwords can still be guessed.

The vendor and every password in the dump are fictional.
