### Spot the Outlier

**Situation:** You've just read how a baseline gets built from a sign-in pattern, a file-access pattern, and a process-execution pattern. Reading the idea and actually telling the two apart on sight are different skills.

**Task:** Three rounds, four items each. Drag every item into the baseline cloud if it fits, or out to the alert zone if it doesn't. The cloud fills in as you go, so by the end of each round you can see the baseline you just built, not just read about it.

Finishing it once counts toward Spot the Outlier on your readiness report.
