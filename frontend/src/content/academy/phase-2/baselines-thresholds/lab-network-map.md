### Map Your Pod

**Situation:** You've just read how your own hosted pod is built: a Windows domain controller and an Ubuntu server, isolated together by their own security group. Reading about it and actually being able to name what's running on it are two different skills.

**Task:** This isn't a quiz, it's a diagram. Drag five real tasks onto the port each one actually uses, drawn right on your pod's two machines. Then drag five connection attempts onto whoever is actually making each one, and watch the diagram draw a real arrow for it, in or out. The lab reads your pod's live status to show what you actually have right now.

Each drop grades itself on the spot, and by the end the diagram shows your pod's whole attack surface in one picture. Finishing it once counts toward Map Your Pod on your readiness report.
