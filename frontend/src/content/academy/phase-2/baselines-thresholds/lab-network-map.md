### Map Your Pod

**Situation:** You've just read how your own hosted pod is built: a Windows domain controller and an Ubuntu server, isolated together by their own security group. Reading about it and actually being able to name what's running on it are two different skills.

**Task:** Name the port behind five real tasks in your pod, then trace five connection attempts against the pod's actual isolation rule: allowed, or blocked. The lab reads your pod's live status to show what you actually have right now.

Each round is checked before you move on, and the debrief explains every call. Finishing it once counts toward Map Your Pod on your readiness report.
