### Map Your Pod

**Situation:** You've just read how your own hosted pod is built: a Windows domain controller and an Ubuntu server, isolated together by their own security group. Reading about it and actually being able to name what's running on it are two different skills.

**Task:** Build the diagram yourself. Drag five real tasks onto the port each one actually uses, drawn right on your pod's two machines, each one labeled with its real IP address. Then drag five connection attempts onto whoever is actually making each one, and watch the diagram draw a real arrow for it, in or out.

Each drop grades itself on the spot, and by the end the diagram shows your pod's whole attack surface in one picture. Finish it and the real data behind the diagram follows: the actual DNS records and firewall rules your own domain controller reports right now, not a teaching example. Finishing it once counts toward Map Your Pod on your readiness report.
