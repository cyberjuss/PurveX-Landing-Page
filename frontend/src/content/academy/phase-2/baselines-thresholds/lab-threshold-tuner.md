### Tune the Threshold

**Situation:** You've just read that a threshold is a trade-off, not a constant. Five real cases are plotted on one scale, from a single mistyped password up to a targeted guess against one account.

**Task:** Drag the threshold line and watch which cases would trigger an alert and which wouldn't. Then two challenges ask you to land the line in a specific, defensible place, not just wherever feels right.

Finishing it once counts toward Tune the Threshold on your readiness report.
