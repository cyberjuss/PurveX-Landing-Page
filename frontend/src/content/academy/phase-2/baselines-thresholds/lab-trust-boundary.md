### Lock Every Gate

**Situation:** A chain from an attacker outside your network through to the payroll database, with three gates between them. Right now only the first one is guarded, the way a perimeter model works.

**Task:** Drag a checkpoint onto every gate, including the shortcut that skips straight from the internal network to payroll. Once every gate is locked, see what a perimeter model actually costs you compared to checking at every hop.

Finishing it once counts toward Lock Every Gate on your readiness report.
