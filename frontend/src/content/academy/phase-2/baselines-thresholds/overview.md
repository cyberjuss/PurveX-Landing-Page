### Overview

The last module gave you the mindset, which comes down to checking the baseline before you trust the alert. This module gives you the baseline itself, along with the machinery that turns a mismatch into a working detection. We'll start with your own hosted lab's network, because that mindset is no use on an environment you've never actually looked at.

You'll also be working with things you already know. Your lab pod, the Shift incidents you've worked, and the P1/P2/P3 severities from SOC Mindset all come back here as the material you practice on.
