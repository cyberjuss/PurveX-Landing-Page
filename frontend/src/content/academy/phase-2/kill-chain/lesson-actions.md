### Actions on Objectives
<!-- art: server -->

Actions on Objectives is the final stage, where the attacker does what they came for, whether that's stealing data, encrypting it for ransom or disrupting operations. Every earlier stage existed to make this moment possible. It's also the stage where the cost to the organization is clearest, which is why defenders work so hard to stop the six stages that come before it.

Because attackers want different things, MITRE ATT&CK splits this final goal across more than one tactic. [Collection (TA0009)](https://attack.mitre.org/tactics/TA0009/) and [Exfiltration (TA0010)](https://attack.mitre.org/tactics/TA0010/) cover the theft of data, while [Impact (TA0040)](https://attack.mitre.org/tactics/TA0040/) covers disruption or destruction, such as ransomware encrypting a file share.

Once an incident reaches this stage, the SOC's job shifts from prevention to containment and scoping. There's no next step left to stop, so what matters now is cutting off the access that got the attacker here and finding out exactly what they reached. That's also why every earlier lesson in this phase is about catching an intrusion before it gets this far.
