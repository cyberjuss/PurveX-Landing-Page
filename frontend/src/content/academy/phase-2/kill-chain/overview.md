### Overview

In Week 4, each ATT&CK-tagged Shift alert showed up as its own event. Real intrusions rarely stop at one, though. An attacker who gets a foothold uses it to steal credentials, uses those credentials to escalate privilege, and then covers their tracks and plants a way back in. Each of those moves is a separate technique with its own ATT&CK tag. This week, you'll learn the Cyber Kill Chain, a framework for thinking about that whole sequence. Then you'll go back to tickets you've already worked this phase and reread them as the single chain they really were.
