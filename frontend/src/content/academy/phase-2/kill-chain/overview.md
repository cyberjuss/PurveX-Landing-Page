### Overview

Week 4's ATT&CK-tagged Shift alerts each showed up as their own event, but real intrusions rarely stop at one. An attacker who gets a foothold uses it to steal credentials, then uses those credentials to escalate privilege. With that privilege, they cover their tracks and plant a way back in. Each move is a separate technique with its own ATT&CK tag. This week gives you the framework for thinking about that sequence: the Cyber Kill Chain. It then asks you to reread tickets you already worked this phase as the one chain they actually were.

### Questions Answered in This Week

- What is the Cyber Kill Chain, and what does each of its seven stages represent?
- How do individual ATT&CK techniques you already know chain together into one intrusion?
- What does it look like to work an incident knowing it is one stage of something larger, not an isolated event?
