### Overview

Week 4's ATT&CK-tagged Shift alerts each showed up as their own event, but real intrusions rarely stop at one. An attacker who gets a foothold uses it to steal credentials, then uses those credentials to escalate privilege, then covers their tracks and plants a way back in. Each move is a separate technique with its own ATT&CK tag. This week gives you the framework for thinking about that sequence, the Cyber Kill Chain, then asks you to reread tickets you already worked this phase as the one chain they actually were.
