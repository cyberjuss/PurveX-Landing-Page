### Sources for this week's facts

- **The three-tier SOC analyst structure (Tier 1 triage, Tier 2 investigation, Tier 3 threat hunting):** these titles and duties are not formally standardized by one body; this lesson draws the common industry framing from [Wiz: Tier 1 SOC Analysts — Responsibilities and Career Path](https://www.wiz.io/academy/cloud-careers/soc-analyst-tier-1) and [Radiant Security: SOC Tier 1 vs Tier 2 vs Tier 3](https://radiantsecurity.ai/learn/soc-tier-1-vs-tier-2-vs-tier-3)
- **51% of SOC teams report being overwhelmed by alert volume, and automating triage has not removed the underlying strain:** [Dark Reading: The SOC Autonomy Paradox](https://www.darkreading.com/cybersecurity-operations/the-soc-autonomy-paradox)
- **The risk of an analyst accepting AI-generated conclusions without verifying them:** [EC-Council University: How AI Is Changing the Role of Security Analysts in 2026](https://www.eccu.edu/blog/how-ai-is-changing-security-analyst-roles/)
- **The 5 W's traced to Aristotle's framework for judging an act (what, who, where, why):** [Wikipedia: Five Ws, "History" section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_Ws), with the applied security framing from [SecurityHQ: The What, When, Where, Who, How and Why Behind Security Incidents](https://www.securityhq.com/blog/the-what-when-where-who-how-and-why-behind-security-incidents)
- **NIST SP 800-61 Revision 3 (2025), which supersedes Revision 2 and reframes incident response around the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0:** [NIST: NIST Revises SP 800-61 — Incident Response Recommendations and Considerations](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2025/04/nist-revises-sp-800-61-incident-response-recommendations-and-considerations), [NIST SP 800-61r3, DOI 10.6028/NIST.SP.800-61r3](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-61r3)

- **The move away from "whitelist/blacklist" toward "allow list/deny list" terminology:** [UK National Cyber Security Centre: Terminology — It's Not Black and White](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/blog-post/terminology-its-not-black-and-white)
