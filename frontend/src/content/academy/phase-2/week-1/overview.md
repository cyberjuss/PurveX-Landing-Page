### Malware

Malware is any software written to do harm: steal data, hold it for ransom, or hand an attacker control of a machine. Most incidents an analyst sees start here, so knowing the common kinds and how they behave is the first step to responding well.

This week covers three things:

- The main types of malware and how each behaves
- How malware gets onto a machine in the first place
- What to do when a malware alert fires on an endpoint

You do not need to reverse-engineer malware to work a tier-1 alert. You need to recognize what you are looking at, contain it, and escalate with the right evidence. That is what this week builds.
