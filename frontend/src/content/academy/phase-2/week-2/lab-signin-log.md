### Read the Sign-In Log

**Situation:** Overnight, the PurveX domain controller and MFA service recorded a night of sign-in events. Four incidents are hidden in that log, and the team needs them sorted before the morning standup.

**Task:** Name the pattern behind each incident and find the row that proves it. Then count the log with a short Python script that runs in your browser, and choose the first response for each incident.

Every address in the log comes from a range reserved for documentation, so none of them is a real host. Your first finished score counts toward Log Analysis on your readiness report.
